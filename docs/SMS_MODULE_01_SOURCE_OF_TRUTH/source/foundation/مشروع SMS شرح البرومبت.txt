============================================================
1. PROJECT NAME
============================================================

SMS

School Management System

Arabic concept:

نظام إدارة المدارس

The system is intended to become a real school-management
platform for daily administrative and academic operations.

============================================================
2. THE BASIC IDEA
============================================================

The basic idea is to build one centralized School Management
System that organizes the school's information and operational
processes into one coherent system.

Instead of having:

- separate spreadsheets;
- disconnected applications;
- manually maintained student records;
- isolated academic records;
- disconnected transfer records;
- duplicated student information;
- uncontrolled paper-based history;

the school should have one structured digital system.

The system should provide a single coherent environment in which
authorized school staff can manage students and the academic
structure surrounding them.

The core philosophy is:

ONE SCHOOL
+
ONE SYSTEM
+
ONE CENTRAL DATA SOURCE
+
ONE STUDENT IDENTITY
+
COMPLETE HISTORY
+
CONTROLLED OPERATIONS

============================================================
3. THE PROBLEM WE ARE TRYING TO SOLVE
============================================================

School administration involves many related pieces of information.

A student is not just a name in a table.

The school needs to understand:

- who the student is;
- how to identify the student permanently;
- who the student's parents/guardians are;
- where the student belongs academically;
- which academic year the student belongs to;
- which grade and class the student belongs to;
- what the student's enrollment history is;
- whether the student moved between classes;
- whether the student transferred externally;
- what happened to the student's historical records;
- who performed important operations;
- when those operations happened.

If these areas are managed separately, several problems appear:

- duplicate student records;
- inconsistent student information;
- lost academic history;
- incorrect class placement;
- capacity problems;
- uncontrolled transfers;
- weak accountability;
- difficult reporting;
- dependence on manual work;
- difficulty maintaining historical records.

SMS is designed around solving these problems systematically.

============================================================
4. THE MOST IMPORTANT CONCEPT:
   THE STUDENT IS A PERMANENT IDENTITY
============================================================

One of the fundamental ideas in our study is:

A STUDENT IS A PERMANENT MASTER ENTITY.

The student should have one permanent identity inside the system.

Academic circumstances can change.

For example:

Student
    ↓
Academic Year 2025/2026
    ↓
Grade 5
    ↓
Class 5-A

Later:

Student
    ↓
Academic Year 2026/2027
    ↓
Grade 6
    ↓
Class 6-B

The student is still the SAME student.

We must not create another Student record simply because:

- the academic year changed;
- the grade changed;
- the class changed;
- the student transferred;
- the enrollment changed.

This distinction is fundamental to the entire system.

============================================================
5. STUDENT VS ENROLLMENT VS TRANSFER
============================================================

These concepts must never be confused.

STUDENT:

Permanent identity of the person.

ENROLLMENT:

The student's academic registration in a specific academic
context/year.

TRANSFER:

A controlled operation that changes the student's academic
placement and/or enrollment situation according to approved
business rules.

Conceptually:

Student
   │
   ├── Guardian Relationships
   │
   ├── Enrollment History
   │
   └── Transfer History

Therefore:

Student ≠ Enrollment

Student ≠ Transfer

Enrollment ≠ Transfer

They are related but different concepts.

============================================================
6. THE SYSTEM IS BUILT AROUND RELATIONSHIPS
============================================================

The project is not simply a collection of CRUD screens.

It is a relational business system.

The important relationships include:

Student
   │
   ├──────── Guardian
   │
   ├──────── Enrollment
   │
   └──────── Transfer History

Academic Structure:

Academic Year
      ↓
Stage
      ↓
Grade
      ↓
Class

Enrollment connects the student to the academic structure.

Transfer connects controlled student movement to the existing
student and enrollment history.

This relationship model is one of the most important aspects of
the project.

9. MODULE 01 — STUDENT MASTER DATA
============================================================

The first core concept is the Student Master.

This module represents the student's permanent identity and

The study includes concepts such as:

- Student ID;
- File Number;
- National ID;
- personal information;
- student profile;
- student status;
- documents/metadata where applicable;
- duplicate detection;
- validation;
- audit;
- permissions;
- search.

The important concept is that Module 01 owns the permanent
student identity.

Other modules should reference that identity rather than
recreating it.

10. MODULE 02 — PARENT / GUARDIAN
============================================================

A student may have relationships with one or more parents or
guardians.

Therefore the system should model the relationship explicitly.

Conceptually:

Student
   │
   ├── Guardian A
   ├── Guardian B
   └── Guardian C

The relationship can contain information such as:

- relationship type;
- primary guardian;
- contact information;
- relationship status;
- relevant communication information.

The important architectural idea is:

Guardian information belongs to its own domain.

It should not be duplicated inside every student record.

============================================================

The school needs a formal academic hierarchy.

The studied structure is:

Academic Year
      ↓
Stage
      ↓

      ↓
Class

For example:

Academic Year
2026/2027

Primary

    ↓

Grade
Grade 5

    ↓

This structure is not merely visual.

It provides the academic context used by enrollment and transfer

The hierarchy must therefore be treated as structured data.

============================================================
12. MODULE 04 — STUDENT ENROLLMENT
============================================================

Enrollment represents the student's registration within the
academic structure for a specific academic context.

Conceptually:

Student
+
Academic Year
+
Stage
+
Grade
+
Class
=
Enrollment

This is why Enrollment should not be stored simply as a current
field on the Student.

The system needs historical enrollment.

For example:

Student A

2024/2025 → Grade 4 → Class 4-A

2025/2026 → Grade 5 → Class 5-B

2026/2027 → Grade 6 → Class 6-A

The student remains one person.

The enrollment history records the student's academic journey.

============================================================
13. MODULE 05 — STUDENT TRANSFERS
============================================================

The transfer domain manages controlled student movement.

Two major concepts are distinguished:

INTERNAL TRANSFER

and

EXTERNAL TRANSFER.

Internal transfer means the student remains within the school
but changes academic placement according to approved rules.

External transfer means the student moves to another
school/institution.

The transfer process must preserve:

- student identity;
- enrollment history;
- transfer history;
- audit history.

A transfer must never create a duplicate student.

============================================================
14. INTERNAL TRANSFER CONCEPT
============================================================

An internal transfer may involve movement between:

- classes;
- grades;
- stages;

where permitted by the academic rules.

The process must consider:

- current enrollment;
- destination academic structure;
- capacity;
- authorization;
- effective date;
- reason;
- historical preservation.

Conceptually:

Current Enrollment
        ↓
Transfer Request
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
Review
        ↓
Approval
        ↓
Execution
        ↓
New Academic Placement
        ↓
Historical Transfer Record

The exact business rules are defined by the approved project
specifications.

============================================================
15. EXTERNAL TRANSFER CONCEPT
============================================================

An external transfer represents the student's movement out of
the school to another institution.

The system must preserve the student's historical identity.

The student's previous records should remain historically
traceable.

External transfer is therefore not equivalent to deleting the
student.

The distinction is:

DELETE THE PERSON

versus

CHANGE THE PERSON'S CURRENT SCHOOL STATUS

The project is designed around preserving history.

============================================================
16. HISTORICAL INTEGRITY
============================================================

Historical information is one of the major design principles.

The system should answer questions such as:

- Where was this student enrolled last year?
- Which class did the student belong to?
- When did the student transfer?
- From where?
- To where?
- Who approved the operation?
- When did it happen?
- What was the student's previous academic placement?

Therefore the system must not destroy history simply to represent
the current state.

CURRENT STATE

and

HISTORICAL STATE

are both important.

The system was studied primarily as:

LOCAL LAN
+
MULTI-USER
+
CENTRAL SERVER
+
CENTRAL DATABASE

Conceptually:

                 SCHOOL SERVER

                 Central DB
                      │
               School LAN

          │           │           │
       Computer    Computer    Computer
        Client      Client      Client

Authorized school staff access the same centralized system.

This is intended to avoid disconnected copies of school data.

============================================================
18. LOCAL / OFFLINE OPERATION
============================================================

Normal school operations should not depend on public Internet
availability.

The core system is intended to operate inside the school LAN.

If the Internet connection is unavailable, normal local school
operations should continue.

Optional external services, such as cloud backup, should not
become a dependency for the core school workflow.

============================================================
19. MULTI-USER THINKING
============================================================

SMS is not a single-user spreadsheet replacement.

Multiple authorized users may work with the same school data.

Therefore the system must conceptually support:

- authentication;
- authorization;
- concurrent operations;
- transactions;
- data integrity;
- audit;
- persistent sessions;
- controlled permissions.

This is especially important for operations such as:

- enrollment;
- capacity management;
- transfer approval;
- transfer execution.

============================================================
20. SECURITY CONCEPT
============================================================

Security is part of the system design, not an optional feature.

The system should have:

Authentication
+
Authorization
+
Audit

Authentication answers:

WHO ARE YOU?

Authorization answers:

WHAT ARE YOU ALLOWED TO DO?

Audit answers:

WHAT HAPPENED, WHO DID IT, AND WHEN?

UI restrictions alone are not sufficient.

Important operations must be protected at the server/business
level.

============================================================
21. AUDIT CONCEPT
============================================================

The system needs an audit trail for important operations.

Examples include:

- student changes;
- guardian changes;
- enrollment changes;
- transfer operations;
- approvals;
- sensitive administrative actions.

The purpose is accountability and historical traceability.

The audit system should answer:

WHO?

WHAT?

WHEN?

AND, where required:

WHAT CHANGED?

============================================================
22. BACKUP CONCEPT
============================================================

Because the system contains important school data, backup is
part of the product concept.

The studied strategy is layered.

LEVEL 1
Automatic local backup.

LEVEL 2
External connected backup medium.

LEVEL 3
Optional cloud backup.

The cloud layer is optional infrastructure support.

The school should not lose the ability to operate locally
because the Internet is unavailable.

SMS is intended for daily school-office work.

The UI should therefore be:

- modern;
- professional;
- clean;
- practical;
- fast;
- easy to learn;
- Arabic-first;
- RTL-capable;
- English-capable;
- consistent;
- suitable for non-technical administrative staff.

The system should feel like a professional administrative
application, not a developer-oriented dashboard.

- fast search;
- clear student identification;
- clear forms;
- simple workflows;
- clear statuses;
- useful filtering;
- tables for operational data;
- safe confirmations;
- understandable errors;
- minimal unnecessary clicks.

The system is an operational tool.
